Backgrounds for the Themes store app (1.1.2). Each <name>.png is 320x218, the area a Lua app owns under
the system status bar: a palette PNG (255 colours, dithered), not interlaced, no alpha.

Boards with a status bar (lumen, pearl, black_pearl, icon_grid, grouped_list, dial, streaming) are the
board cropped to rows 22..239. Boards with none (control_tiles, terminal, epaper, bold_blocks,
monster_rpg) are the whole 320x240 board scaled uniformly by 218/240 to 291x218 and centred (x offset 14),
the side margins filled with the board's ground (control_tiles: the wallpaper at full width).

Blanked on every face, for you to draw: the owner, the badge ID, the Wi-Fi/online state, the battery and
Wi-Fi glyphs, page dots and page counts, the OS version. ALSO blanked, since the app has no permission
for them: the !LAMPs figure AND its "!LAMPs" / "!LAMPs balance" label, and the Wi-Fi network name. Nothing
neutral is baked in their place: put the badge ID or "Online"/"Offline" there, or leave them empty.
Titles such as "Wi-Fi", "Quests", "MY BADGE", "BADGE CARD", menu entries and hints are baked in.

Per field: the board's sample text, its box in FINAL image coordinates (x, y, w, h), alignment, colour,
opacity if any, the board's face and size after scaling, and the nearest Lua face (mono 12, 14, 20, 32).
ICON lines are removed glyphs/dots with their boxes. chip-box is a chip's background rect, still baked.
